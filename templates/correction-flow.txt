ROLEPROGRESS — CONCEPT PREVIEW
1. Inspect the exact claim, source, scope and date.
2. Preserve the original claim.
3. Worker states the correction and optional non-sensitive supporting context.
4. Set pending review; assign reviewer and due date.
5. Reviewer compares evidence and asks for clarification or records a reasoned outcome.
6. Accepted correction changes the record; competence still needs appropriate assessment.
7. Rejected correction includes a reason and a separate appeal/reviewer path.
8. Notify the worker only in a future approved operating service. This demo sends nothing.
