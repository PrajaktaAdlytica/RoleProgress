ROLEPROGRESS — RESEARCH-STAGE PILOT BRIEF
Buyer: employer HR/talent development director with open adjacent roles. Workforce board funding requires concrete employer interview commitment. Workers participate with correction rights.
Vision: workers can challenge their skill evidence and pursue credible transitions employers consider.
Mission: connect supervised evidence, available training and openings to measurable mobility or placement.
Five separate paid-offer tests: Skills Desk, Skills Review, Skills Proof, Skills Signal, Skills Report. Start Skills Proof only after valid Skills Review evidence.
Hypothesis: $4,000 for 25 participants and employer pathway workshop; not an accepted offer.
Demand test: interview 10 employers/supervisors; run one manually facilitated employer/training-partner cohort; secure funding and written interview consideration.
Build after paid sponsorship + valid assessment + available training. Pivot to supervisor assessment if pathways already exist. Stop if no hiring slots or persistent biased inference.
Expand only after placement/mobility lift at acceptable assessment cost. Count paid programs and renewals, not the total labor market.
Evidence: reference data and incumbents established; skill-to-job impact unvalidated. Review mapping accuracy, taxonomy licenses, bias, consent, credential efficacy, HR procurement ownership.
MVP exclusions: HRIS import, automated hiring ranking, credential issuance, payment collection, production matching.
