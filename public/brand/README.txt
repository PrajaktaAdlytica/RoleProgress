ROLEPROGRESS — PROGRESS FOLIO VECTOR SET

Selected concept: option 3. Three sculpted page/leaf forms, with open seams and a rising center. The symbol was manually rebuilt with cubic Bezier paths; it is not a raster image embedded in SVG.

Horizontal and stacked logos: primary (copper symbol/ink lettering), ink, copper, and reversed white.
Standalone symbol: copper, ink, white.
Standalone wordmark: copper, ink, white.
Favicons: SVG, 16/32/48px PNG, multi-size ICO.
Application icons: SVG, 192/512px PNG and 180px Apple touch icon.

All lettering is outlined, based on Newsreader at weight 550 with kerning. The font license is included. No installed fonts are needed to display these SVGs. Keep the master files; do not stretch their aspect ratio.

Use primary on white/light backgrounds; white on dark backgrounds. Leave at least one-third of the symbol width clear around the mark. Use favicon.svg for tiny applications; its seams are optically widened. At very small sizes use the symbol alone. Branding is a design asset, not a claim of trademark registration or clearance.

Reproduction scripts: scripts/build-brand.py. The original raster concept remains archived separately. Brand colors: copper #95543c, ink #29212c, white #ffffff. Supporting surfaces: sage #e0e8dd, lilac #e8e2f0, rose #f0dfd7, mist #e6edf0.
